# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community.
Contributors should assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus on
the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without
consent.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's private contact channels. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict
participation, or take other action necessary to keep the project safe.
